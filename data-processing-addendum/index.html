Data Processing Addendum

For businesses that put their customers' personal data into Rebuildify.

Scope and roles

This addendum applies when Rebuildify ("Rebuildify") processes personal data for a business that uses the platform ("Customer"). The Customer is the controller, or the processor acting for its own customers, and Rebuildify is the processor, or sub-processor. It forms part of the Terms of Service and any signed agreement.

What we process, and why

We process the personal data the Customer and its users put into the platform, such as contact details, messages, call recordings and transcripts, bookings, payment records and documents, only to provide the service and to follow the Customer's documented instructions. The Customer's use of the platform's settings and features is its instruction.

Our duties

  • Process the data only on the Customer's instructions, unless the law requires otherwise, and tell the Customer if we think an instruction breaks the law.
  • Keep each business's data apart from every other business's data.
  • Make sure people who can reach the data are bound to keep it confidential.
  • Apply the security measures on our Security page.
  • Help the Customer answer requests from people who want to see, correct, export or erase their data. The platform includes tools for export and erasure; the record that a call took place stays.
  • Tell the Customer without undue delay after we become aware of a breach affecting its personal data.
  • Help with data protection impact assessments and consultations with authorities, to the extent the law requires and we hold the relevant information.

Sub-processors

The Customer agrees that we may use the sub-processors listed on the Sub-processors page. We will update that page before a new one starts processing Customer data, so the Customer can object. Where a Customer connects its own provider account, that provider is the Customer's own supplier, not our sub-processor.

International transfers

Where personal data is transferred to a country that needs a legal mechanism for the transfer, the parties will use the mechanism that the law allows, such as standard contractual clauses. The hosting region for an agency's workspace is recorded in the order form; at present only the United States is operated.

Deletion and return

When the agreement ends, the Customer can export its data. After that we delete it, except where the law requires us to keep it. The period for deletion is the one set out in the order form or agreement.

Audits

On reasonable written request we will give the Customer the information needed to show we follow this addendum, and will allow audits as the signed agreement provides.
