What is billed separately

Plan prices cover what each plan lists. Calls beyond the included amount are billed per call, and telephone minutes, phone numbers, text messages and AI usage, are billed separately as they are used.

The 21-day free trial

Every plan starts with a 21-day free trial that we set up with you. After the trial, billing is monthly in advance. Cancel at any time before the trial ends and nothing is charged. Once a charge has been made it is not refunded.
