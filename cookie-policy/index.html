Cookie Policy

What is stored in your browser when you use this website and the platform, and what is not.

What a cookie is

A cookie is a small piece of information a website asks your browser to keep. Related things, such as local storage, work in a similar way. This policy uses the word cookie for all of them.

This marketing website

The enquiry and strategy-call booking form on this website does not keep what you type in your browser. Your details are sent to Rebuildify so we can reply and arrange your call.

This website does not load advertising or tracking pixels.

Signing in to the platform

When you sign in to a Rebuildify workspace, the platform sets a session cookie so it knows you are signed in. It is needed for the service to work, is marked secure when the site is served over HTTPS, and is not used to follow you around other websites.

Sites our customers build

Businesses can build websites and funnels with Rebuildify and can add their own tracking codes and pixels. The platform shows those codes to a visitor only after the visitor agrees. Each business is responsible for the cookies on its own sites and for its own cookie notice.

Your choices

You can delete cookies or block them in your browser settings. If you block the sign-in cookie you will not be able to sign in to the platform. Where we show a consent choice, we honour it, and refusing is as easy as accepting.
