Accessibility Statement

We want everyone to be able to use this website.

What we aim for

We aim for this website to work for people using a keyboard, a screen reader, a phone or a large screen, and to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. We have not had an independent accessibility audit, so we do not claim full conformance.

What we do

  • Native buttons, links, labels and form fields wherever possible.
  • Every control has a name that says what it does.
  • Everything can be used with the keyboard, with a visible focus indicator.
  • The menu opens and closes with the keyboard and the Escape key.
  • A skip link to the main content.
  • Colour is not the only signal, and text is designed to meet AA contrast.
  • Motion respects the "reduce motion" setting on your device, and nothing blocks reading or using the page.

Known limits

Some parts of the platform behind the sign-in are still being checked. If something does not work for you, we want to know.

Tell us about a problem

Email ceo@rebuildify.agency and tell us the page, what you were trying to do, and the device or assistive technology you use. We will reply and, where we can, fix it or offer another way to get what you need.
