Refund and Cancellation Policy

How we bill, how to cancel, and when money is not returned.

Payments are not refundable

Once a charge has been made, it is not refunded. This covers plan subscriptions, extra seats, campaign work, engineering time and set-up. When a plan starts or a seat is set up, we immediately allocate computing resources, telephone numbers and engineering time that cannot be recovered. Charges that were made by mistake are the one exception, see Errors on our side below.

Pass-through costs

Telephone minutes, number rentals, carrier registrations for business texting, text messages and AI usage are costs charged to us by the providers that supply them. They are recorded against your account as they are used and cannot be recovered once they have been incurred.

How billing works

A plan is billed in advance for each billing period, usually one month, and renews for the next period automatically until you cancel. The price you see for the plan is the price you are charged. Usage that is billed separately is charged as described in the pass-through costs above.

The 21-day free trial

Every plan starts with a 21-day free trial that we set up with you. After the trial, billing is monthly in advance. If you cancel at any time before the trial ends, nothing is charged. Once a charge has been made, the no-refund rule above applies.

Cancelling

You can cancel at any time by writing to ceo@rebuildify.agency from the address on your account. Your access continues until the end of the period you have already paid for, and no further charge is made after that. Cancelling does not return amounts already paid. You can ask for an export of your data before the end date.

Errors on our side

If we charged you by mistake, for example twice for the same thing, tell us and we will correct it.

Payments your customers make through the platform

When your customers pay you through a payment provider you have connected, refunds and disputes between you and your customers are yours to handle. Rebuildify gives you the tools to issue full or partial refunds with a reason and to answer disputes, but does not take part in the decision.
