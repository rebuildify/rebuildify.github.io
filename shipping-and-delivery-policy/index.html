Shipping and Delivery Policy

Rebuildify is online software. Here is how you get access, and what to do if you cannot.

Nothing is shipped

Rebuildify sells software and services that you use over the internet. We do not ship physical goods, so there are no delivery charges, couriers or delivery addresses.

How you get access

When your plan or trial is agreed with us, your workspace is set up. You then sign in to it in a web browser, and on the mobile app where it applies to your plan. Access is tied to the business account that signed up and the people that business adds. Every plan starts with a 21-day free trial that we set up with you; after the trial, billing is monthly in advance. Once a charge has been made it is not refunded, as the Refund and Cancellation Policy explains.

Services we do for you

Where your plan includes work done by our team, such as set-up or campaign management, what is included and when it is delivered is written in your order form or agreement.

Services from other providers

Some features need an account with another provider, such as a phone carrier, an email service or a payment provider. Their own checks and approvals can take time, and that time is outside our control. We will tell you what is needed from you.

If you cannot get in

If you cannot sign in or something you paid for is not available, email ceo@rebuildify.agency with the name of your business and what you see. We will look into it and reply. How payments and cancellations work is set out in the Refund and Cancellation Policy.
