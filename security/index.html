Security

What we do to protect your data, and what we do not claim.

Each business is kept apart

The platform is built so each business sees only its own conversations, customers, knowledge, credentials and recordings. Agencies see their client workspaces, and agency staff see only the clients assigned to them.

Access control

  • Access by role, and by mailbox for email.
  • Supervisor listening, whispering and joining are separate permissions, and each access is logged.
  • Changes to ad accounts and other sensitive settings are recorded.

Keys and secrets

Provider keys and tokens that customers connect are sealed when they are stored and are never shown back after they are saved. Keys are entered only through a dedicated pop-up or page, never inline in a form.

Messages and webhooks

Webhooks from providers are checked for a valid signature, and duplicate events are ignored. A duplicate outbound call is stopped, and a call or charge with an unclear result is listed for a person to settle rather than guessed.

Data location

A data-region setting exists for agencies. Only the United States is operated today; India is listed but cannot be selected yet.

Customer data rights

A customer's call data can be exported and erased on request. The record that a call took place stays.

What we do not claim

We do not claim any security certification or audit report on this page. No system is perfectly secure. If you need our answers to a security questionnaire, email ceo@rebuildify.agency.

Report a vulnerability

If you think you have found a security problem, email ceo@rebuildify.agency with what you found and how to reproduce it. Please do not access other people's data or disrupt the service while testing.
