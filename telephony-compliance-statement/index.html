Telephony Compliance Statement

The rules the dialer and AI calling features are built to help you follow.

Our position

Rebuildify has no tolerance for unlawful robocalls, fraudulent schemes, harassment or unauthorised spam. Customers must follow the Telephone Consumer Protection Act, the rules of the Federal Communications Commission and the laws of the places they call, and the Terms of Service make that a condition of use.

Controls in the product

  • Calling hours, allowed destinations and recording-notice settings for each business.
  • An internal do-not-call list; a stop request is honoured immediately.
  • A record of where each customer's calling permission came from, kept with the call.
  • Premium and high-risk destination prefixes blocked unless the business allows them.
  • A limit on abandoned calls that the business sets, as a share of answered calls. Advanced dialling is off until the business switches it on, and dialling slows and stops by itself when the limit is reached.
  • Limits on attempts per customer and per number over a time window.
  • AI calls announce that they are AI at the start.

What Rebuildify cannot do for you

The controls help; they do not replace the customer's own duty to have permission before calling. Rebuildify may suspend an account that exceeds legal abandonment limits or that carriers repeatedly flag as spam.

Carriers

Calls go out through the carrier account a customer connects, or through numbers supplied through the platform. Each carrier's own acceptable-use rules also apply.
