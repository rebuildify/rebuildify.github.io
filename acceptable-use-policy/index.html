Acceptable Use Policy

The rules for using calling, messaging, email, AI and publishing features.

The idea

Rebuildify is for contacting people who expect to hear from you, about things they care about, in the ways the law allows. If you use it for anything else, we may stop the service.

You must not use Rebuildify for

  • Unlawful robocalls, spam, harassment, threats or fraud.
  • Contacting people who have not given the permission the law requires, or who have asked you to stop.
  • Pretending an AI is a human, or hiding that a call is automated or recorded.
  • Cloning a voice without the speaker's consent.
  • Collecting or selling people's mobile numbers or other personal information to third parties for their marketing.
  • Hiding who you are, or showing a caller identity you are not entitled to use.
  • Content that is illegal, deceptive, hateful, sexually exploitative, or that infringes someone else's rights.
  • Attacking, probing or overloading the platform or other customers, or trying to reach another business's data.
  • Reselling carrier or AI usage outside the agency and rebilling features the platform provides.

Calls

  • Call only within the hours and to the destinations you have set and the law allows.
  • Keep an up-to-date do-not-call list and honour a stop request at once.
  • Keep the share of abandoned calls from predictive dialling within the limit that applies to you. Rebuildify lets you set the limit and stops dialling when it is reached.
  • Tell people when a call is recorded, where the law or your settings require it.

Text messages and WhatsApp

  • Send only to people who have agreed to receive that kind of message from you. The SMS and Messaging Terms explain the wording and records that are needed.
  • Honour STOP and HELP.
  • Register your business and campaign with the carriers before texting from a number that needs it, and do not send content outside what you registered.

Email

  • Send only to people you have a lawful reason to contact.
  • Include a working unsubscribe link and a real sender identity, and remove people who unsubscribe or complain.
  • Do not use purchased or scraped lists.

Content you publish with Rebuildify

You are responsible for the sites, forms, funnels, reviews, posts and ads you publish. Review requests must go to every customer alike, whatever they are likely to say; you must not filter who gets asked. Reviews must be real.

What we may do

We may hold or block messages that break these rules, limit or suspend numbers, campaigns or accounts, remove published content, and report unlawful activity to the authorities. Repeated carrier spam flags or breaches of calling limits may lead to suspension. Where we can, we will tell you why.

Reporting a problem

To report abuse of Rebuildify, email ceo@rebuildify.agency.
