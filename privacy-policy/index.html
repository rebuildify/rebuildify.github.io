Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, who sees it, and what you can ask us to do.

Who we are

Rebuildify ("Rebuildify", "we", "us") runs the Rebuildify platform and this website. Our address is Gupta Niwas, Tikuriya Tola, Chauraha Lakhan, Sohawal, Satna, Madhya Pradesh 485001, India. You can write to us at ceo@rebuildify.agency.

Two different roles

When you visit this website or ask us for a strategy call, we decide why and how your information is used. We are the controller of that information, and this policy describes it.

When a business uses Rebuildify to talk to its own customers, that business decides why and how its customers' information is used, and Rebuildify processes it on the business's behalf. The terms for that are in our Data Processing Addendum. If you are the customer of a business that uses Rebuildify, please ask that business first about your information.

What we collect from this website

  • What you type into the strategy-call booking form: your name, business email, telephone number, organisation, the time you pick, and any message you write.
  • Basic technical information that your browser sends to any website, such as the page you asked for, so the site can be delivered and kept secure.
  • Nothing is kept in your browser by the enquiry or booking form. See the Cookie Policy for the full list of what the site does and does not store.

What we use it for

We do not sell, lease, trade or monetise your personal or organisation information to brokers or data aggregators.

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange your call.
  • To run, secure and fix the website.
  • To meet our legal duties.

Information in the platform

Businesses that use Rebuildify store their own customer records, messages, call recordings, transcripts, bookings and documents in their own workspace. Each business's data is kept apart from every other business's data. Rebuildify uses that information only to provide the service to that business, as set out in the Data Processing Addendum.

Who we share information with

We share information only with the service providers we need to run the website and the platform. They are listed on the Sub-processors page, with what each one does. Where a business connects its own carrier, email, payment or other account, information goes to that provider because the business asked for it.

Where information is kept

Rebuildify is currently operated in the United States. The platform has a data-region setting for an agency; India is listed there but cannot be selected yet. The region that applies to your information is recorded in your order form.

How long we keep it

We keep information for as long as we need it for the purpose it was collected for, and for as long as the law requires. For a business's workspace, the retention periods are those set out in its agreement with us and in the workspace's own settings. A request to erase a person's call data removes the data; the fact that a call took place stays in the record.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see the information we hold about you, to have it corrected, to have it erased, to receive a copy in a common format, to object to some uses, and to withdraw consent you gave. To use any of these, email ceo@rebuildify.agency and say what you want. If your request concerns a business that uses Rebuildify, we will pass it to that business, because it controls that information.

You can stop marketing messages at any time: reply STOP to a text, use the unsubscribe link in an email, or tell us on a call.

Security

We protect information with the measures described on our Security page. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot promise that nothing will ever go wrong.

Children

Rebuildify is a business service. It is not meant for children, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we will change the date at the top of the page. If a change is significant we will tell the businesses we have a contract with.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please write to us first at ceo@rebuildify.agency. You may also complain to the data protection authority where you live.
