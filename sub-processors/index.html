Sub-processors

Who helps us run Rebuildify, and who a business connects for itself.

How to read this list

A sub-processor is a company that handles personal data for us when we provide the service. The list has two parts. The first part is providers Rebuildify itself uses when it supplies a service. The second part is providers a business connects with its own account and its own keys; those are the business's own suppliers, not ours.

Providers Rebuildify uses when it supplies the service

The hosting company and region that run the Rebuildify servers are named in your order form. The full list of where each provider processes data is being compiled and is not yet published here.

  • Stripe and Skydo: take and manage payment for Rebuildify plans and platform billing. Svix: checks the signature on incoming carrier and payment notifications.
  • Telnyx, Plivo, SignalWire, Twilio: telephone and text carriers. Rebuildify uses them to supply numbers, calls and texts when a business chooses a Rebuildify-supplied carrier instead of its own.
  • Resend and Telnyx: email delivery when a business chooses a Rebuildify-supplied email sender.
  • Google (Gemini), Groq and OmniRoute: AI language processing for features such as AI replies, summaries and call analysis. DuckDuckGo and Wikipedia: web look-ups an AI reply may use.
  • ElevenLabs and Dograh: AI voice generation and voice-agent processing. LiveKit: real-time audio rooms for calls in the browser.
  • Firebase (Google): stores application data for parts of the workspace. Expo: delivers push notifications to the mobile app. Cloudflare: DNS look-ups and cache clearing for published pages.

Providers a business connects with its own account

A business can connect its own account and keys for the following kinds of service. Data sent to them is sent because the business chose to connect them, under the business's own agreement with that provider.

  • Language models and AI voice: OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, Groq, Together, Mistral, xAI, DeepSeek, OpenRouter, Fireworks, Cerebras, Cohere, Perplexity, Moonshot, Qwen, Zhipu, NVIDIA, SambaNova, Hyperbolic, or any OpenAI-compatible server the business names.
  • Speech and voice: Deepgram, AssemblyAI, ElevenLabs, Cartesia, Sarvam, Fish Audio, Murf, Inworld, Resemble, Rime, Smallest.ai, Microsoft Azure, Dograh.
  • Telephone and text carriers: Telnyx, Plivo, SignalWire, Twilio.
  • Email: Resend, Mailgun, Telnyx, and connected Google and Microsoft mailboxes.
  • Payments: Stripe and other payment accounts the business connects.
  • Calendars, WhatsApp, Meta (Facebook and Instagram), Google Business Profile and domain registrars, where the business connects them.

Changes to this list

We update this page before a new sub-processor starts handling customer data. Businesses with a signed Data Processing Addendum can object to a new sub-processor as that addendum describes. Questions: ceo@rebuildify.agency.
