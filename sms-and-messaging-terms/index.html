SMS and Messaging Terms

Consent, opt-out and the information every recipient and every sender needs to know.

Who this is for

These terms cover text messages (SMS and MMS) and WhatsApp messages sent through Rebuildify. They are for recipients, who should know what they agreed to, and for businesses that send messages, who must meet the consent rules below.

Consent, for people who receive messages

You receive messages from a business only if you agreed to receive them from that business, for example by ticking an unticked box or typing a keyword. Agreeing to receive messages is not a condition of buying anything. Rebuildify delivers messages for the business; the business is the sender.

Example opt-in wording

A business using Rebuildify must show clear wording at the point it collects a mobile number. Wording like this meets the requirement, with the business's own name and details filled in:

"By giving us your mobile number and ticking this box, you agree to receive text messages from [Business name] about [what the messages are for, such as appointment reminders and offers]. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel at any time. Reply HELP for help. Agreeing is not a condition of any purchase. Mobile numbers and opt-in consent are not shared with third parties for their marketing. See our Privacy Policy and Terms at [link]."

STOP and HELP

  • Reply STOP to a text to stop all marketing texts from that sender. A stop request, including a plain-words request such as "don't text me again", is honoured at once, across every channel the business uses.
  • Reply HELP to a text to get help and the sender's contact details.
  • After you reply STOP you may get one message confirming you have been removed. After that, no more marketing texts.

Message frequency, rates and delivery

How often you receive messages depends on what you signed up for, and the business tells you at sign-up. Message and data rates from your mobile carrier may apply. Carriers do not guarantee delivery, and a message can be delayed or blocked. Rebuildify shows the honest status of each message, such as queued, sent, delivered or failed, and does not report an open rate for texts.

No sharing of mobile numbers for marketing

Rebuildify does not sell or share mobile numbers or opt-in consent with third parties for their marketing. Numbers are used only to deliver the messages the business asked Rebuildify to send.

Rules for businesses that send

  • Get and keep proof of each person's consent: who, when, where and the wording shown. Rebuildify records the consent source with each contact.
  • Keep marketing and operational messages separate; consent for one is not consent for the other.
  • Every marketing text ends with a way to stop, such as "Reply STOP to opt out."
  • Respect quiet hours and sending limits. Rebuildify applies the ones you set.
  • Register your business and your campaign with the carriers before texting from a local number, and complete toll-free verification before texting from a toll-free number. Send only what you registered.
  • Do not send messages about prohibited subjects or to numbers you bought, rented or scraped.

WhatsApp

WhatsApp messages go through the official WhatsApp Business platform using the business's own account. A business may start a conversation only with an approved template, to a person who has agreed to be contacted on WhatsApp, and may reply freely inside the reply window the platform allows.
