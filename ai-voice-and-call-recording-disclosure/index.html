AI Voice and Call Recording Disclosure

You are told when you are talking to an AI, and when a call is recorded.

You will be told it is an AI

Calls made or answered by a Rebuildify AI voice agent say at the start that the caller is an AI. Rebuildify does not design its voice agents to pretend to be human, and businesses may not use the platform to do so.

A person can take over

A call with an AI agent can be handed to a person: to a named person, a department or a queue, depending on the business's hours and who is available. A business can also have a person take over from the AI in the middle of a call.

Recording and transcripts

A business can turn call recording and transcripts on. When it does, the call settings include a recording notice, and the business decides when and how it is played, in line with the law where it operates. Recordings and transcripts belong to the business whose call it was, are kept apart from other businesses' data, and are used to provide the service to that business, such as searchable transcripts, call summaries and review by supervisors.

Supervisors

Supervisors at a business can listen to a live call, whisper to a rep, or join, each as a separate permission, and every access is logged.

Cloned voices

A voice can be cloned only from a sample the speaker has consented to, through the business's own provider key.

Your choices

  • Tell the caller on the call, or the business afterwards, that you do not want to be recorded or called again. A stop request is honoured immediately and added to the do-not-call list.
  • Ask the business for a copy of your call data, or to erase it. Rebuildify provides the business with export and erase tools; the record that a call took place stays.
  • To ask Rebuildify about a call, email ceo@rebuildify.agency and say which business called you and when.

What AI can get wrong

AI voice agents can mishear or give a wrong answer. Businesses are responsible for what their agent says, and for checking its set-up on test calls before real customers hear anything.
